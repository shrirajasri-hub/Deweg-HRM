Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for leave?Go to Leave Application → pick a category and dates → days auto-calculate → submit. You can speak your reason using the microphone button.
Who approves my leave?Your reporting manager configured in your employee master record receives the request instantly.
Where can I see my past leave?Leave Report shows all your requests with status. Managers can view the full team history.
Forgot your password?Contact IT Support (Ext. 4021) — credentials are issued by HR only.
